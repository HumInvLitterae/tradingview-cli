This release matures the explicit Desktop-backed quote-data source so agents
can distinguish successful `qsd.rtc` readback from bounded source
unavailability without mixing scanner, chart, or WebSocket evidence.

## Highlights

- Added `contract_version: "quote_data.v1"` to
  `tv quote <SYMBOL> --source quote-data` success payloads and structured
  unavailable details.
- Added `source_availability` readback so quote-data results report whether
  the bounded source was available, whether `rtc` was observed, and whether
  raw frames were included.
- Added machine-readable quote-data unavailable diagnostics, including
  `unavailable_reason`, `timed_out`, `next_action`, and public-safe wait
  summary counters.
- Added `quote_data.session_readback` to normalize TradingView-provided
  session spelling without inferring a session TradingView did not report.
- Kept quote-data explicit and separate from scanner `extended_hours`,
  selected-chart main-series quote data, and `--source auto`.

## Behavior Notes

- `tv quote <SYMBOL> --source quote-data` remains a bounded Desktop-backed
  WebSocket quote-data readback source. It does not switch chart symbols and
  does not fall back to scanner or chart-source quote.
- Structured unavailable results mean the quote-data source was unavailable
  during the bounded wait. They do not mean the symbol has no market price.
- Scanner-backed `tv quote <SYMBOL>` and `tv quotes <SYMBOL>...` remain
  Desktop-free scanner REST reads. They can be delayed; inspect `time`,
  `update_mode`, and `delay_seconds` when timing matters.
- `tv quote <SYMBOL> --source chart` remains selected-chart main-series data
  and does not expose scanner-style extended-hours fields.
- Premarket-specific quote-data live evidence remains deferred until the
  relevant market phase.

## Packaging

Release archives include the `tv` binary, README, changelog, license,
user-facing agent guides, and runtime-oriented skills only. Development-only
skills remain excluded.
