# v0.31 retained-backlog product selection

This note records the source-and-workflow comparison after the one-minute bars
and Desktop-free bars diagnostics slices. It does not authorize implementation.

## Decision

Promote no additional product candidate for v0.31. The concrete downstream
blocker was bounded one-minute date-range bars, and that contract is implemented
and handed off. The release now has one user-facing historical-data capability
and one actionable diagnostic improvement. Adding another feature without its
required trigger would weaken the evidence-first release boundary.

Proceed to the v0.31 completion and architecture audit.

## Comparison

Event date ranges still lack a concrete consumer contract beyond the current
earnings/dividend-shaped `events.v1`; a complete calendar also lacks a stable
source. Historical requests above 5,000 returned bars have a documented
non-overlapping-window workflow, and no reviewed workload shows that workflow is
insufficient. Additional intraday timeframes have no current downstream demand.

Pine named new-save still lacks a reachable, post-checkable naming-dialog
boundary. Screener column reset lacks a durable default-column authority or
visible reset action. Alert edit, pause, and resume require new mutation
ownership and verification research. None meets the roadmap's promotion gate.

These candidates remain retained or deferred with their existing triggers. This
no-promotion decision does not classify them as permanently impossible.
