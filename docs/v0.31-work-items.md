# v0.31.0 ordered work inventory

This inventory turns `docs/v0.31-roadmap.md` into an explicit execution order.
Placement does not authorize network access, production changes, dependencies,
version bumps, or remote operations.

## Global rules

- Preserve `bars.v1`, source labels, error classification, and the 5,000
  returned-bar safety cap.
- Do not infer exchange calendars, entitlement, or expected closures.
- Keep public and private evidence separate. Never record downstream symbols,
  prices, case dates, membership, receipts, or authority digests here.
- Add no hidden fallback to Desktop chart data, Replay, scanner, quote-data, or
  another provider.
- Every implementation slice receives a self-contained ExecPlan and focused
  review. Do not push without explicit owner direction.

## Maintained defers and exclusions

Do not raise the 5,000-bar cap, widen additional intraday date ranges, add a
calendar source, add retry, or introduce shared process ownership without a
separate reviewed plan.

Pre-dispatch retry, subprocess latency attribution, shared session/broker,
public recovery metadata, generalized waits, finite-`f64` right-offset
restoration, width-derived drawing geometry, and Windows MSIX/AUMID launch
activation retain their existing evidence or policy triggers. They are not
ordered v0.31 work.

Renderer lifecycle and indicator search remain current-build no-go/deferred
until a concrete render-linked transition mechanism or relevant Desktop build
change appears.

MCP, daemon operation, cookie/session import, login automation, trading bots,
automatic source mixing, ranking, scoring, recommendation, and buy/sell output
remain excluded.

Preserve both local stashes without applying or dropping them unless the owner
explicitly changes that decision.
